Calypso · incognito mode

Ask without a trace.

Calypso is the incognito mode. It swaps the window for a thin dark strip. Turn it down until it's barely there, type your question, press Return, and it disappears until the answer's ready. It's the mode I use most.

Menu bar › Turn On Calypso

Tucked away.

Switch it on from the menu bar, or type /calypso. The screen frosts over and a lilac flower opens in the middle, then folds shut into a bud as two green leaves close around it. The bud tucks up into a little black island at the top of the screen. That's the one loud bit. After that everything goes quiet, and the menu-bar icon turns into a pair of sunglasses so you know it's on.

Turning it off runs it backwards: the bud drops out of the island and opens up again. You can switch the animation off in Settings › Calypso.

⌥Space

A strip instead of a window.

A thin dark bar shows up over whatever you're doing. It has no buttons, no chat history and no shadow, only the words you type.

Settings › Calypso

Turn it down to almost nothing.

You choose how see-through it is, anywhere from 5% to solid, and the island goes just as faint. Near the bottom of that range you can only just make it out.

It's discreet, not invisible. If you share or record your screen, people can still see it.

Return

Press Return and it's gone.

The bar vanishes while Calyx works. The island at the top of your screen says it's thinking, then shows a few words about the answer once it's on your clipboard.

Questions

        

          Isn't this just ChatGPT?
          
Kind of. It uses the same AI models, so it isn't claiming to be smarter. The difference is how you use it: you screenshot the question, press one more key, and the answer is pasted in, without a ChatGPT window or any copying back and forth. For longer conversations I'd still use the proper apps.

        

        

          Do I need an API key?
          
For Claude or OpenAI, yes. Get one from Anthropic or OpenAI, paste it into Settings, and click Test key. The provider charges you for what you use. Apple's offline model doesn't need a key.

        

        

          Will it work on my Mac?
          
As long as it's an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 or later. Offline AI needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled.

        

        

          Why does it need Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions?
          
Screen Recording lets you attach what's on your screen. Accessibility lets Calyx paste into other apps for you. You can skip either permission and the rest of the app still works. You can check permissions under Settings › Shortcuts.

        

        

          My Mac says Calyx "can't be opened."
          
macOS can block apps downloaded outside the App Store. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, then choose Open Anyway for Calyx. You should only need to do it once.

        

        

          Can other people see the Calypso bar?
          
If they're looking at your screen, yes, though at low visibility it's faint. It also shows up if you share or record your screen. Calypso keeps your screen uncluttered. It isn't built to hide anything from anyone.
