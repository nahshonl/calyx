Questions

        

          Isn't this just ChatGPT?
          
Kind of. It uses the same AI models, so it's not claiming to be smarter. The difference is how you use it. You're stuck on a coding question, you screenshot it, get a hint, and get back to coding. No separate ChatGPT window and no copying everything back and forth. For longer conversations, I'd still use the proper apps.

        

        

          Do I need an API key?
          
For Claude or OpenAI, yes. Get one from Anthropic or OpenAI, paste it into Settings, and click Test key. The provider charges you for what you use. Apple's offline model doesn't need a key.

        

        

          Will it work on my Mac?
          
As long as it's an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer) on macOS 14 or later. Offline AI needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled.

        

        

          Why does it need Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions?
          
Screen Recording lets you attach what's on your screen. Accessibility lets Calyx paste into other apps for you. You can skip either permission and the rest of the app still works. You can check permissions under Settings › Shortcuts.

        

        

          My Mac says Calyx "can't be opened."
          
macOS can block apps downloaded outside the App Store. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, then choose Open Anyway for Calyx. You should only need to do it once.
